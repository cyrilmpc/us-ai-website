📝 Application Guide — Step by Step

Start 6–12 months before your target intake (February and July are the main ones).

📅
1. Pick your intake & courseFebruary (Semester 1) and July (Semester 2) are the main intakes; some unis offer November too. Check CRICOS registration — only registered courses qualify for a student visa.
🗂️
2. Prepare documentsAcademic transcripts, passport, English test result, CV/statement of purpose, work references (if any), and financial evidence for living funds.
🗣️
3. Meet the English requirementTypically IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall (no band below 6.0) for most degrees; medicine, law and teaching ask for more. PTE, TOEFL and Cambridge are usually accepted.
🎯
4. Satisfy the Genuine Student (GS) requirementShow your course fits your study history, that you can fund your stay, and that you genuinely intend to study. The GS replaced the old GTE in 2024.
📜
5. Get your CoE & apply for the visaAccept your offer, pay the deposit, receive the Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), then lodge Student visa (subclass 500) online via ImmiAccount. Fee: AUD 2,500 from 1 July 2026 (non-refundable).
🛡️
6. Arrange OSHC & book travelOverseas Student Health Cover is mandatory — arrange it before visa grant and keep it your whole stay (roughly AUD 500–700/yr for singles). Book flights after your visa is granted.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to study in Australia in 2026?
International bachelor's tuition typically runs AUD 20,000–45,000 per year, master's AUD 22,000–50,000, and PhD AUD 18,000–42,000. You must also show about AUD 29,710 per year in living funds for the visa, plus the Student visa (subclass 500) application charge of AUD 2,500 from 1 July 2026.
What is the Student visa (subclass 500) fee in 2026?
From 1 July 2026 the primary-applicant application charge is AUD 2,500 (AUD 2,050 for ELICOS and non-award sectors). It is non-refundable even if the visa is refused. Confirm the current amount on homeaffairs.gov.au before you lodge.
Can international students work while studying in Australia?
Yes — student visa holders may work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session, and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks.
What English score do I need to study in Australia?
Most universities ask for IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall (no band below 6.0) for undergraduate and postgraduate coursework, with higher scores for medicine, law and teaching. PTE, TOEFL and Cambridge are usually accepted too.
When are the intakes for Australian universities?
The two main intakes are February (Semester 1) and July (Semester 2). Some universities also offer a smaller November intake. Apply 6–12 months early.
What is the Genuine Student (GS) requirement?
The GS requirement replaced the old GTE in 2024. You must convince the Department of Home Affairs that you genuinely intend to study — through your study history, course choice, finances and ties to home. It is assessed with your visa application.
Can I stay and work in Australia after graduation?
Yes — the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) lets eligible graduates live and work in Australia after finishing their course. Post-study work streams generally allow 2–4 years depending on your qualification level and region.
What is OSHC and is it mandatory?
Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is mandatory health insurance for all student visa holders. You must arrange it before your visa is granted and keep it for your whole stay. Typical singles cover costs roughly AUD 500–700 per year.

Fees, visa charges and requirements change — always confirm the latest figures on studyinaustralia.gov.au and homeaffairs.gov.au before you apply. Figures above reflect 2026 policy as reported by multiple visa-advisory sources.
