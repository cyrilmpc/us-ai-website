❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which sewing machine is best for a complete beginner?

The Brother XM2701 and Janome Mod-19 are ideal first machines — simple dial controls, automatic needle threader, and enough stitches for real projects without overwhelming settings.

Can I do embroidery on a regular sewing machine?

Regular machines can do decorative stitches, but true embroidery needs an embroidery unit — the Brother SE700 does both sewing and 4x4 embroidery, while the PE900 is embroidery-only with a larger 5x7 field.

What software works with Brother embroidery machines?

The SE700 and PE900 work with Brother's free Design Database Transfer software and the Artspira mobile app for wireless design transfer. PE-Design 11 adds full design creation and is sold separately.
