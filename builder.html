❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Free Starter plan really free? What do I get?

Yes, $0. You get a 1-page website built by our team with standard delivery. No payment and no card needed — just log in and describe your website.

How does it work? Do I need to know how to code?

No coding needed. Log in, fill the request form (website/business name, description, plan), and our team builds your website in about 10 minutes — Premium Shop plans jump the queue with priority building. Watch the live status box for progress.

Why is login required?

So we can deliver your website to the right account and you can request edits and features anytime from the same box.

What is the difference between the four plans?

Free Starter ($0): 1-page website, standard delivery. Standard ($17.85): up to 5 pages, contact form, 1 free revision. Business ($35.71): up to 10 pages, Urdu/English content, WhatsApp button, 3 revisions. Premium Shop ($71.42): online shop, unlimited pages, priority ~10 minute delivery, 1-month support.

How do I pay for a paid plan?

Paid plans use JazzCash: transfer the amount shown (≈ Rs at Rs 280 = $1), then enter the 11-digit TID in the payment step. Delivery starts after payment confirmation.

Can I change my website later?

Yes. Paid plans include free revisions (Standard: 1, Business: 3; Premium Shop: 1-month support). Just open the builder box again and request edits anytime.

How fast will I get my website?

Most websites are delivered within ~10 minutes of your request. Premium Shop plans jump the queue with priority building. If your request is complex, the status box keeps you updated.
