🇵🇰 Pakistan's World Cup Story

🏆 1992 — Cornered Tigers

Pakistan were almost out after five group games, then won everything. Inzamam's semi-final blitz vs New Zealand and Wasim Akram's magic in the final vs England — Imran Khan lifted the trophy in Melbourne.

⚡ 2009 — T20 Champions

Runners-up in 2007, champions in 2009: beat Sri Lanka in the Lord's final. Shahid Afridi was player of the match with an unbeaten fifty and 1 wicket.

🥇 2017 — Champions Trophy

Not a World Cup, but Pakistan's finest ODI day since 1992: Fakhar Zaman's century and a 180-run win over India in the Oval final.

🥈 2022 — So Close

Reached the T20 World Cup final in Melbourne but lost to England. Sam Curran's 3/12 decided a low-scoring final.

📊 Records

🏏

Most ODI WC Runs

Sachin Tendulkar — 2,278 runs across six World Cups (1992–2011). Six hundreds, fifteen fifties — the eternal record.

🎯

Most ODI WC Wickets

Glenn McGrath — 71 wickets (1996–2007). Metronome accuracy across four tournaments, best figures 7/15.

⚡

Fastest WC Century

Glenn Maxwell — 40 balls vs Netherlands, Delhi 2023. 106 off 44 balls, the fastest hundred in World Cup history.

💥

Highest WC Total

South Africa 428/5 vs Sri Lanka, Delhi 2023 — three centuries in one innings (de Kock, van der Dussen, Markram).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which country has won the most ODI Cricket World Cups?

Australia, with 6 ODI World Cup titles (1987, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2015 and 2023). India and West Indies have 2 ODI titles each.

Which teams have won the T20 World Cup?

India (2007, 2024), Pakistan (2009), England (2010, 2022), West Indies (2012, 2016), Sri Lanka (2014) and Australia (2021).

When did Pakistan win the Cricket World Cup?

Pakistan won the ODI World Cup in 1992 under Imran Khan, beating England in the Melbourne final, and the T20 World Cup in 2009 under Younis Khan at Lord's.

Who has scored the most runs in Cricket World Cup history?

Sachin Tendulkar with 2,278 runs across six ODI World Cups (1992-2011) — the all-time record.
