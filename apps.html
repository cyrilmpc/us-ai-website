❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are the app templates really free?

Yes. Type your app idea in the search box and download free ready-made templates instantly. Login is required even for free downloads.

What is the difference between a template and a custom app?

Templates are ready-made apps you download instantly. Custom apps are built exactly the way you describe — your design, your features, your name on it.

How long does a custom app take?

Standard custom apps target ~15 minutes. A big or complex app may take a little longer. The exact plan price is shown when you choose a plan.

How do I pay for a custom app?

Paid custom apps use JazzCash: transfer the shown amount and enter the 11-digit TID before the order is placed.

Why is login required?

Both free and paid use login so your templates and custom-app orders are saved to your account and you can get them again anytime.
