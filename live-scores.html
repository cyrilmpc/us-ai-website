❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is the live score really live? How often does it update?

Yes, free forever. Use the "↻ Refresh now" button anytime for the freshest score; the page refreshes automatically in the background so Live Now matches stay current.

What do the three groups mean?

🔴 Live Now = matches playing right now. 🕐 Upcoming Matches = fixtures with date and time. ✅ Recent Results = finished matches with final scores.

What is the "Full scorecard" button?

It opens the detailed match page (ball-by-ball / full stats) in a new tab so you can dig deeper without losing this page.

What is the Cricket Centre?

Tap any cricket card’s details to see venue, date and time, live score, toss result and both squads. Details load only when you tap, keeping it fast and free.

Do I need an account or payment?

No. Everything on this page is free with no login — scores, schedules, results and Cricket Centre details.

How do I read the tennis scores?

Live tennis cards show which set is currently in play (e.g. “2nd set”). If a set score isn’t available yet, the card shows ⏳ Score updating — tap “📊 Full scorecard” on any tennis card for the full set-by-set score. A player shown as “TBD” means their draw opponent isn’t decided yet.
